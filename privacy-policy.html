Privacy Policy — Betting Calculators

Last updated: May 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Betting Calculators Chrome extension ("the extension"). The extension provides offline betting math tools (parlay payout, odds conversion, hedge, market hold and expected value).

Data we collect

None. The extension does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or usage data. All calculations happen locally in your browser. The extension does not use cookies, analytics, tracking, or remote servers.

Permissions

The extension requests no special browser permissions. It cannot read your browsing history, your tabs, or the content of any web page.

Third parties

The extension contains a link to the developer's website (correctbetting.com). Visiting that link is optional and governed by that website's own policy.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the developer through the support link on the Chrome Web Store listing.
